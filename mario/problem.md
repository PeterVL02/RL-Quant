# Capture The Flag I

Source: QuantProf #613 ([reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeXy1k3MdhL/), @quantprof_dot_org)

## Problem

Mario and the princess start **1 meter** apart.

Over the next **three days**, each day Mario receives a number chosen uniformly
at random from **[0, 1]** and moves toward the princess by that distance.

On **exactly one** of the three days, *after seeing the number he receives*, he
may choose to move **twice** that distance instead.

Mario **succeeds** if he reaches or passes the princess by the end of the third
day. Otherwise, he loses.

**Question:** What is Mario's probability of success if he plays optimally?
